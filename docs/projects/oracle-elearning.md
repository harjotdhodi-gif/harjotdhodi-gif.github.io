# Oracle eLearning & Localization Program

![Oracle eLearning and localization program](/img/portfolio/oracle-elearning.svg)

**Organization:** Oracle  
**Role:** Information Development Operations  
**Period:** Sep 2015 - Apr 2018  
**Portfolios:** Drug Safety, Clinical, and Healthcare

## Scope

I delivered **40+ eLearning courses and short videos** across **9 English titles and 7 Japanese titles** for Safety, Clinical, and Healthcare products.

## Responsibilities

- eLearning production and short instructional videos
- Translation and localization coordination
- Technical and linguistic review cycles
- Vendor coordination across the United Kingdom, India, and Romania
- LMS assignments
- Instructor-led training event administration
- Customer-query resolution
- Documentation of recurring production issues in a shared knowledge repository

## Value demonstrated

The program combines technical communication with **learning design, multilingual delivery, vendor coordination, quality review, and operational knowledge capture**.
