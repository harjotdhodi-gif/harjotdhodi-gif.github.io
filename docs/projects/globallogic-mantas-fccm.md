**Role:** Consultant — Technical Writing Group  
**Period:** Dec 2005 – May 2012  
**Teams:** India and United States  
**Products / solutions:** Mantas Behavior Detection Platform, Fraud Detection, Oracle Financial Crime and Compliance Management (FCCM) Solutions

## Context

This engagement covered long-running enterprise documentation for financial-services software across distributed India and United States teams. The work spanned multiple release cycles and combined hands-on technical writing with team coordination and client-facing documentation operations.

## What I delivered

- Delivered enterprise product documentation across **10+ major release cycles** in financial-services software and other domains.
- Produced documentation for the **Mantas Behavior Detection Platform**, **Fraud Detection**, and **Oracle Financial Crime and Compliance Management (FCCM) Solutions**.
- Managed a small writing team and coordinated work across US and India stakeholders.
- Led knowledge-transfer sessions covering documentation processes and authoring tools.
- Maintained consolidated weekly status reporting for the documentation function.
- Represented the technical writing team in client project reviews.

## Documentation leadership model

The role combined delivery and coordination. In addition to authoring and release support, I helped structure team communication, knowledge transfer, progress reporting, and stakeholder coordination so the documentation function remained aligned with product delivery.

## Value demonstrated

This case demonstrates sustained enterprise documentation experience in financial-services software: **major-release execution, product-domain continuity, writing-team leadership, distributed collaboration, knowledge transfer, and client-facing reporting**.
